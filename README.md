## 📌 Problem Statement

**SIH 26033: Multiple Intermediaries Reduce Farmers' Earnings and Increase Consumer Prices.**

Traditional agricultural supply chains involve multiple intermediaries between farmers and consumers. This increases consumer prices while reducing the actual earnings of farmers.

## 💡 Proposed Solution

**AgriConnect** is a smart digital marketplace designed to connect farmers and Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) directly with consumers, retailers, and bulk buyers.

The platform reduces unnecessary intermediaries by enabling farmers to list agricultural products, set competitive prices, and receive orders directly from buyers.

It also integrates AI-powered demand forecasting and smart logistics management to improve agricultural distribution and delivery efficiency.

### ✨ Key Features

- 🌾 Direct farmer-to-consumer connections.
- 💰 Transparent pricing and real-time product availability.
- 🤖 AI-based demand forecasting and market analysis.
- 🚚 Smart logistics and delivery route optimization.
- 📦 Digital payment and order tracking.
- 👨‍🌾 Support for individual farmers and FPOs.

## ⚙️ Technical Approach

### Implementation Workflow

1. Farmers and FPOs register on the platform.
2. Farmers upload agricultural products and set prices.
3. Consumers and bulk buyers browse products and place orders.
4. The system connects buyers directly with suitable farmers.
5. AI analyses historical sales data to forecast future demand.
6. The logistics module identifies suitable delivery options and optimizes routes.
7. Buyers track deliveries and farmers receive digital payment updates.

## 🔍 Feasibility and Viability

### Feasibility

AgriConnect can be developed using existing web technologies, cloud infrastructure, AI/ML algorithms, and digital payment systems. The platform can initially be implemented in selected agricultural regions and gradually expanded.

### Potential Challenges

- Limited digital awareness among farmers.
- Transportation and storage difficulties.
- Fluctuating agricultural prices.
- Maintaining product quality during delivery.
- Building trust between farmers and buyers.

### Proposed Solutions

- Provide a simple, multilingual user interface.
- Partner with local FPOs and logistics providers.
- Display transparent and regularly updated prices.
- Introduce buyer reviews and farmer verification.
- Use AI-based demand forecasting to reduce wastage.
